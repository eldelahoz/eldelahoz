### <img src="./assets/icons/bot.svg" width="22"> AI-Augmented Engineering

- **Harnesses:** isolated, reviewable work units with plan + todo. Auditable by design.
- **Skills:** brainstorm → plan → TDD → debug systematically → verify before merge.
- **Plugins & protocols:** code, docs and tools exposed as structured context. No copy-paste.
- **Memory:** decisions and gotchas stay in the repo (ADRs, lessons), not in chat.
- **Humans decide:** AI proposes, tests argue, humans merge.

> I use AI to raise the quality bar, not to skip it.

### <img src="./assets/icons/drafting-compass.svg" width="22"> How I design solutions

- **Problem & constraints first:** understand the business rule, its edge cases and what "done" means before any code.
- **Domain modeling:** entities, value objects and aggregates (DDD building blocks) business language in code, not CRUD shapes.
- **Architecture:** ports & adapters with clean layers domain at the center, infrastructure at the edges, dependencies pointing inward.
- **Patterns where they pay:** Strategy for interchangeable business rules, Decorator for cross-cutting concerns, CQRS when reads and writes scale differently, idempotent steps for batch jobs.
- **Decisions on record:** one ADR per meaningful choice, TDD as executable spec, docs-as-code so the next dev doesn't need me on call.

> Simple beats clever. If the next maintainer can't follow it, it's not finished.
